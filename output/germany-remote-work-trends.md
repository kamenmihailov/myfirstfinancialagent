# Remote Work Trends in Germany
*Research date: 2026-05-15*

## Adoption Stats

- **~24%** of German employees worked from home at least part-time (Aug 2025) — just above the EU average of 23%
- Hybrid is now the dominant model: only **24%** of home-office users work *exclusively* remote (down from 40% during COVID in 2021)
- Average: degree holders work from home **~1.6 days/week**
- Germany trails the EU leaders: Netherlands (52%), Sweden (46%), Luxembourg (43%)

## By Sector

- **Information/tech**: 82% of companies offer home office at least 1 day/week
- **Manufacturing**: 48% — higher than expected for an on-site-heavy industry
- Over 70% of employees work remotely fully or part-time when all flexible arrangements are counted

## Future Outlook

- Home office availability expected to rise to **88%** in the information economy and **57%** in manufacturing by mid-2026
- ~2/3 of information-sector companies expect 20%+ of staff working from home at least weekly by June 2026
- Remote/hybrid is now a talent retention tool — companies with rigid in-office mandates report difficulty attracting top tech talent
- Aligns with Germany's sustainability goals (reduced commuting = lower carbon emissions)

## Key Takeaway

Germany is firmly in a stable hybrid era. Full remote is shrinking as a share; hybrid with 1-2 remote days/week is the new normal, especially in tech and knowledge work.
